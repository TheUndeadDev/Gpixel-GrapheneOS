01 / GET COMFORTABLE

First steps

A calm start is a good start. The setup flow will walk you through the basics.

  1. 01

    Connect and update

    Join Wi-Fi, finish setup, then open Settings → System → System update. Install available updates and restart if asked.

  2. 02

    Choose a strong screen lock

    Use a PIN you do not use elsewhere. A longer PIN is harder to guess. Fingerprint unlock is optional; your PIN remains important.

  3. 03

    Bring over what matters

    Copy photos, contacts and other essentials from your old phone during setup or later. Some apps may need you to sign in again. Check that your important data arrived before wiping the old phone.

  4. 04

    Add apps at your comfort level

    GrapheneOS works without Google apps. If an app needs Google Play, the official sandboxed Google Play install is available from the GrapheneOS App Store; it runs without special privileges.

!

Already started setup? No problem. You can change most choices later in Settings. Do not follow installation steps for this phone. If setup warns that the bootloader is unlocked, pause here and ask the person who gave you the phone to resolve it before adding personal data.

02 / THE EVERYDAY

Everyday use

A few small differences, then it feels like a phone.

A

Browsing

Vanadium is the included browser. Bookmarks, passwords and site settings live in its own settings.

B

Apps

Try the GrapheneOS App Store for system apps and sandboxed Google Play. You can install apps without giving them every permission they ask for.

C

Photos & files

Use the camera and file apps as usual. Decide where copies of important photos and documents should live.

D

Calls & messages

SIM and eSIM setup depends on your carrier. If you need to move an eSIM, check with the carrier before removing it from the old phone.

What if an app says Google Play services are missing?

Some apps depend on Google Play. You can install sandboxed Google Play from the GrapheneOS App Store, then install the app from Google Play. You can also choose a different app if you prefer not to use it.

03 / YOUR CONTROLS

Privacy, your way

You do not have to turn everything off. Give each app only the access that makes sense to you.

◉

Review app permissions

Settings → Apps → See all apps → choose an app → Permissions. Revisit these whenever you like.

⌖

Location access

Choose whether an app gets location, and whether it can use precise location. Many apps work without it.

▣

Camera and microphone

Android shows indicators when apps use these. You can also control access in system settings and Quick Settings.

↻

Keep the phone current

Install GrapheneOS and app updates when they are offered. Updates are an important part of keeping the device maintained.
